60 years of 3D modeling, and the hardest part never changed... until now. 🧊➡️✏️

1963: Sketchpad and a light pen. 1972: Catmull's polygon hand. 1975: the Utah teapot. 1995: the first fully computer-animated movie. The pictures got unbelievably good, but artists still click and drag vertices one by one.

Now: draw a sketch and Vi3W turns it into a 3D model in seconds, with auto-rig, animation and Roblox/Unity export. Every model at the end was made in Vi3W.
