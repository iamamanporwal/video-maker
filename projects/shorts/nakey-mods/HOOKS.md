## Why these five

1. **01 Among Us before Among Us** is the single most-rewatched stretch. It opens on a claim that makes viewers argue ("TTT came first") and ends on the "impostor among us" sing-along.
2. **02 Fortnite only exists because…** is a recursive chain joke. Comments fill with corrections ("you missed Battle Royale the film"), and that drives reach.
3. **03 Zero coding → first mod** is the sharpest spike in the video. "Zero experience" is relatable and the payoff is a pure emotional release. It's also the best brand bridge: no code, no Blender.
4. **04 Parry a mini nuke** cold-opens on the nuke (visual absurdity), then rewinds to how they built it.
5. **05 Thomas the Tank everything** cold-opens on the strongest visual gag in the video. The thesis line comes back as the button.
