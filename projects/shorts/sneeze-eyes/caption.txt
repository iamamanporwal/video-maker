What happens if you sneeze with your eyes open? 👀🤧

Every kid on the playground said your eyeballs would pop out. Here's what actually happens (and the real danger nobody warned you about).

Facts: six extraocular muscles hold each eye; high-speed imaging (Tang et al., Univ. of Alberta) measured sneezes at ~4.5 m/s (~10 mph); tracheal tear from a stifled sneeze: BMJ Case Reports, Dec 2023 (Dundee, Scotland).
