# The Studio That Got Its Time Back

## Logline

A 3D studio loses itself inside a maze of slow tools, until one idea travels from sketch to usable model in three clicks.

## Story spine

- Once upon a time, artists made worlds but spent most of their time waiting.
- Every day, a simple idea passed through a maze of tools and revisions.
- Until one day, the maze became three clicks.
- Because of that, the artist could iterate while the idea was still alive.
- Until finally, one small robot became a game, a product, and a film character.
- And ever since then, time belongs to the artist again.
