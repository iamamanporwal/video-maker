# GTA 6 launch-day downloads: facts used (checked 2026-11-12)

| claim in the video | source | status |
|---|---|---|
| Game needs "up to 212 GB" of free space | Rockstar Newswire pre-load post (via GameRant, gtaboom, VICE) | VERIFIED. Actual download ~209.8 GB per RockstarINTEL; a PSN manifest lists 223 GB. We only say 212. |
| Pre-load opened Nov 10, nine days before the Nov 19 launch | Rockstar Newswire (GameRant, gtalab) | VERIFIED |
| "Broke the internet" / "one British network had its busiest day ever" | Virgin Media O2: Nov 10 was its busiest day on record, +3.8 % over the previous peak; first-hour traffic +51.6 % vs the week before (ISPreview, Yahoo Tech). YouFibre blamed the downloads for an overloaded gateway in Yorkshire. | VERIFIED. "Broke the internet" is hook hyperbole, backed on screen by the O2 record. |
| "Analysts expect 25 million copies on day one" | GamesRadar analyst round-up (25-30M); Piper Sandler ~45M; Newzoo 51M in week one | HEDGE: always "analysts expect", on-screen "25-45 million". No official number. |
| "5 billion gigabytes at once" | 25M × 212 GB = 5.3B GB | NAPKIN MATH, labelled "if everyone downloads". Many copies are discs or already pre-loaded. |
| PS5 unlocks at midnight local time | Rockstar pre-load schedule (GameRant, gtaboom). Xbox pre-load opened 12:00 AM GMT worldwide. | VERIFIED for PS5. We only name PS5. |
| "Copied onto thousands of servers, yours comes from one near your city" | How CDNs work in general (ISPreview notes CDNs cache content close to users) | GENERIC. Which CDNs Sony/Microsoft use for GTA 6 is not public, so we name none. |
| "Already on your console, locked. Midnight turns the key" | Pre-loaded files wait for a release-time check before the game will start | VERIFIED in general terms. No claim about encryption or file sizes. |
| Day-one patch | Not announced. ISPreview expects one may come. | HEDGE: "Unless there's a day one patch." |

Not used: Sensor Tower pre-order estimates (7.7M vs 10.3M, two conflicting figures), 223 GB package size, Warzone 2.0 comparison (21 Tbps peak on VMO2, 2022).
