TITLE:
GTA 6 is lying to you in every single frame 👀

DESCRIPTION:
At 30 fps a console gets 33 milliseconds to build every picture. So open worlds cheat. 4 ways:

1️⃣ LOD: far-away stuff uses simpler models (35,000 triangles up close, almost nothing far away)
2️⃣ Culling: behind you or behind a building? Not drawn
3️⃣ Streaming: the map loads in tiles around you
4️⃣ Instancing: 100 pedestrians = 1 model drawn 100 times

Rockstar hasn't published GTA 6's tech. These are the standard open-world tricks, shown with our own 3D demo.
