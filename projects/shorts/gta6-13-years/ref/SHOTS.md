# Shot catalogue — gta6-13-years (9:16 crops)

Contact sheets: `ref/sheets/<id>_NN.jpg`. GTA V sheets sample every 2.5 s and GTA 6 sheets every 3 s, each frame labelled with its timestamp. A `t` value below is a frame that was looked at. The cut it belongs to usually runs about ±1 s around it, so scrub the boundaries before locking an edit.
`cx` = suggested horizontal crop centre (0 = left edge, 1 = right edge) for a 9:16 window cut from 16:9 (the window is about 32% of the frame width).
Use the `ref/h264/` copies for editing because the originals are AV1/VP9.
