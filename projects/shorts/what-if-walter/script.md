# What if Walter White never had cancer? (script, story-architect)

**Logline:** Take the cancer away and Walter White lives thirty more safe years. He just never comes alive.

## Spine
- Once upon a time: a 50-year-old chemistry teacher in Albuquerque works a second job at a car wash.
- Until one day: inoperable lung cancer. He becomes Heisenberg.
- But what if: the cough was just a cough.
- Because of that: no ride-along, no Jesse. Jane, Wayfarer 515 and Hank all live.
- Because of that: Walt keeps grading papers and swallowing it.
- The turn: the cancer didn't create Heisenberg. The pride did (Gray Matter, $5,000). Cancer only gave permission.
- Until finally: he lives to 80. Safe, respected, forgotten. His own confession: "I was alive."
- Ever since: two lines. Two years alive vs thirty years never alive. Which would you choose?

**Emotional question:** would a safer life be a better one? **The one image:** a short blazing amber line beside a long grey one.

## Fact list (show canon, checked 2026-11-04)
- Diagnosed with inoperable lung cancer days after his 50th birthday; Albuquerque chemistry teacher. IMDb pilot summary, Wikipedia "Pilot (Breaking Bad)".
- Second job at a car wash; DEA ride-along with Hank where he sees ex-student Jesse escape through a window (pilot).
- Sold his Gray Matter share for $5,000; the company is later worth billions. Sources differ on the exact figure
  ($2.16B in-show NYT column, $2.6B elsewhere), so the script says "billions" only. Slashfilm / Cheatsheet on Gilligan's explanation.
- Jane Margolis dies of an overdose while Walt watches (S2 "Phoenix"); her father's grief leads to the Wayfarer 515 mid-air collision (S2 "ABQ").
- Hank is killed in the desert ("Ozymandias"). Jesse is held captive by Todd's crew (S5).
- Quote, "Felina" (S5E16): "I did it for me. I liked it. I was good at it. And I was really... I was alive." (punctuation varies by transcript).
- Walt dies on his 52nd birthday, hence "2 years" on the A line. Speculation (clearly framed as "what if"): Jesse "probably" lives, Walt lives to 80.
