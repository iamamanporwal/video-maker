What if you stayed awake for 11 days? 😵‍💫

In 1963, a 17-year-old named Randy Gardner tried it for a science fair, and the results got weird fast. The record still stands.

This whole video was made with the Vi3W workflow → vi3w.in (link in bio)

#shorts #sleep #didyouknow #science #3danimation #ps2

Facts: Randy Gardner stayed awake 264.4 hours (Dec 1963 – 8 Jan 1964) using Coca-Cola, loud music and cold showers; moody and uncoordinated by day 3; on day 4 he mistook a street sign for a person and believed he was football player Paul Lowe; failed a count-down-by-sevens test at 65; slept ~14 hours after; developed insomnia later in life. Guinness stopped monitoring sleep-deprivation records in 1997, citing the dangers.
