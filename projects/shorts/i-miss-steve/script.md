# I miss Steve: script (story-architect)

**Logline:** Fired from his own company, Steve Jobs bet on "computer cartoons" and proved 3D could move the world.
Thirty years on, 3D is still locked behind complexity, and Vi3W wants to open it.

**Spine:** Once upon a time a founder built Apple → until one day (1985) he was fired → because of that he started
NeXT and bought Lucasfilm's graphics lab → because of that he bankrolled years of losses → until finally Toy Story
(1995) → and ever since, 3D has been an art form, but still for experts only → Vi3W.

**The one image:** colour flooding into the picture, and the paper bars going black, on the words "Toy Story".
**The turn:** "And yet..." (thirty years later, the dream is still out of reach).
**Callback:** the 1984 Gotfryd photo opens the film in B&W and returns in full colour on "I miss Steve".

| The quote | NYT Magazine, 30 Nov 2003 |

Changes from the user's draft: "the first fully 3D animated film ever made" became "the first feature film ever made
entirely on a computer". "He created [the 3D industry]" was cut as an overclaim. The script also adds the 1985
firing and NeXT opening that the brief asked for.
