# Direction (film-director)

**Look:** a 1980s archival documentary that bleeds into colour as the dream comes true.
**Rules:** the past is textured (grain, dust, gate weave, VHS tears at the cuts). The present is clean. Paper-white
letterbox bars are the archive. They go black at the payoff and stay black.

## Shot list
Built in node/remotion/src/shorts/IMissSteve.tsx. Every boundary is a VO word anchor, see `anchors()`.

1. VHS power-on → B&W close-up (1984), push in. "1985. Steve Jobs was fired..."
2. Hard cut on "from": the Gotfryd Mac portrait.
3. Reagan award (Feb 1985); the frame darkens on "finished." Film-burn VHS tear.
4. NeXT entrance → hard cut on "NeXT" to the cube. Dymo labels: Cupertino 1985 / NeXT 1985 / San Rafael 1986.
5. Pixar Image Computer pan → hard cut on "Pixar" to the gate letters.
6. Extreme close-up of the face on "People laughed" → CRT wireframe teapot on "computer cartoons".
7. Years flip 1986 → 1995, decelerating to land on the spoken "1995".
8. On "Toy": flash, colour bloom, bars to black, projector-beam title card "Toy Story / November 22, 1995".
9. The CRT teapot, now rendered: glossy red on a checkerboard ("made entirely on a computer").
10. The Pixar gate again, now in colour; Jobs on stage, 2005.
11. "And yet...": a hand-drawn idea buried under 24 tool panels, then caged on "trapped". Serif statements.
12. The quote over the 2010 portrait, revealed word by word on the narrator's timing.
13. Vi3W logo → the real app: sketch → image → 3D → browser.
14. Shutter sound in silence → the 1984 photo returns in FULL colour: "I miss Steve."
15. A gallery of 9 real Vi3W models → end card "In memory of Steve Jobs, 1955 – 2011".
